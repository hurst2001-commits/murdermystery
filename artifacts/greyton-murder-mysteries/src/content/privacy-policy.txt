Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 28 September 2026
Greyton Murder Mysteries respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when using our website, making an enquiry, registering for an event, or purchasing a ticket.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.
1. Information We Collect
When you use the Greyton Murder Mysteries website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:
- Your name and surname
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Booking or event information
- Number of participants in your booking
- Messages or enquiries submitted through the website
- Information necessary to process or manage your booking
- Any other information you choose to provide to us
We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit the website, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited and general website usage information.
2. How We Use Your Information
We may use your personal information to:
- Process and manage bookings
- Respond to enquiries
- Send booking confirmations and important event information
- Communicate changes relating to an event or booking
- Provide customer support
- Improve our website and visitor experience
- Maintain the security and functionality of the website
- Meet our legal, accounting or regulatory obligations
- Send marketing communications where you have consented to receive them
We will not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to those for which it was collected unless permitted or required by law.
3. Online Bookings and Payments
Where bookings or payments are processed through a third-party payment provider, certain information may be provided directly to that provider.
Greyton Murder Mysteries does not intentionally store complete debit or credit card details on its website unless specifically stated during the payment process.
Payment providers may process personal information according to their own privacy policies and security procedures.
4. Cookies and Website Analytics
Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function correctly, understand how visitors use the website and improve the overall user experience.
Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser.
Depending on the services used on the website, cookies may also be created by third-party services such as analytics, embedded content, maps, social media platforms or advertising services.
You can restrict or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.
5. Marketing Communications
If you subscribe to receive news, event announcements or promotional information from Greyton Murder Mysteries, we may use your contact details for this purpose.
You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe option provided in the communication or by contacting us directly.
Operational messages relating to an existing booking may still be sent when necessary.
6. Sharing Your Information
We do not sell or rent your personal information.
Personal information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website or provide our services. These may include website hosting providers, payment processors, booking platforms, email providers, analytics providers and other technical service providers.
Information may also be disclosed where required by South African law, a court order or another lawful regulatory process.
7. Protection of Personal Information
We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.
However, no website, online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
8. Retention of Information
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or for as long as required by applicable legal, accounting or regulatory requirements.
Information that is no longer required will be deleted, destroyed or de-identified where reasonably practicable.
9. POPIA
Greyton Murder Mysteries aims to process personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).
Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:
- Request access to personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
- Request deletion of information where legally permitted
- Object to certain processing of your personal information
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where there is another lawful basis for retaining it.
10. Children's Privacy
Our website and events may be accessible to families, depending on the particular event.
Where personal information relating to a child is required, it should be provided by or with the appropriate involvement or consent of a parent or legal guardian where required by law.
We do not knowingly use children's personal information for unrelated marketing purposes without appropriate authorisation.
11. Third-Party Websites
Our website may contain links to external websites or services.
Greyton Murder Mysteries is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.
12. Your Responsibility
Please ensure that the information you provide through our website is accurate and up to date.
If you provide personal information relating to another person, you should ensure that you are authorised to provide that information where required.
13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, legal obligations or business practices change.
The latest version will be published on this website together with its updated revision date.
